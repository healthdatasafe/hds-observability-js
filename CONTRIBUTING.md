# Contributing

Issues, fixes and proposals are welcome. This repository is published by
[Health Data Safe](https://www.healthdatasafe.org), a Swiss non-profit foundation.

## Licence of contributions

A contribution is released under the licence that covers the files it changes
(see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). You keep the copyright in your contribution; Health
Data Safe does not ask you to assign it.

## Sign off your commits (DCO)

Every commit in a pull request must carry a sign-off line:

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.org>

`git commit -s` adds it. By signing off you certify the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): you
wrote the change, or otherwise have the right to submit it under the
repository's licence. Pull requests with commits that are not signed off are
not merged.
